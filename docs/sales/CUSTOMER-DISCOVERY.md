# Customer Discovery — Interview Script

> Used during Phase 6 concierge sales to test the Radar hypothesis *before*
> building the product. Goal: one hour, one real buyer, verbatim notes. The
> gate ([VALIDATION-GATE.md](./VALIDATION-GATE.md)) only counts **recorded**
> evidence — record every answer.

## Before the call

- Fill the customer row in [PROSPECT-DATABASE.md](./PROSPECT-DATABASE.md).
- Read the customer's website and pick one believable use case to reference.

## Script

### 1. Open (2 min)

> I research Indian fintech companies — payments and regulated fintech — so
> teams that sell to, partner with, or advise them can find the right companies
> and see when something important changes. No pitch yet; I want to understand
> how you work today.

### 2. Current workflow (15 min)

1. How do you find the Indian fintech companies you work with today?
2. What was the last time you needed a list of companies by [category / licence]?
3. How did you build it — tools, spreadsheets, Inc42, Tracxn, RBI lists, calling around?
4. How long did it take, and what did you miss?
5. When you follow a company, what makes you pay attention?
6. How do you learn about a change (licence, funding, acquisition, people)?

### 3. The job to be done (10 min)

> "Find the exact Indian fintech companies relevant to me and keep me informed
> when something important changes."

7. When in the last month did this matter most?
8. What was the outcome you were really trying for? (result, not task)
9. What makes this hard today?

### 4. Validation (10 min)

10. What would a weekly change report have saved you in the last month?
11. If I gave you a verified list of payment aggregators with licence statuses
    and sources today — what's the first thing you'd do with it?
12. Who else in your team would use this?

### 5. Price & intent (10 min)

13. What do you pay today for company data (Inc42 Pro ~₹1,499/mo, Tracxn, etc.)?
14. If this removed [their pain], would you pay ₹[X]/month? (name a number from
    PRICING-EXPERIMENTS.md)
15. Would you take a first deliverable by invoice this month?

### 6. Close (3 min)

- Record a verbatim quote they'll let you use.
- Note the warm-referral question: *"Who else has this problem?"*

## After the call

- Log answers into the prospect row the same day.
- Classify: `paying design partner` / `later` / `not a fit` / `referral`.
- If paying: deliver per [RESEARCH-OUTPUT-TEMPLATE.md](./RESEARCH-OUTPUT-TEMPLATE.md)
  within the agreed window and record the gate evidence.

## Rules

- Ask, don't pitch.
- Never invent an answer; write "no answer" when silence.
- Do not sell during the discovery call — the deliverable sells.
