# India FinTech Company Directory (2026)

> Research compilation: 2026-08-14. Compiled from 35+ web searches across
> Tracxn, Inc42, YourStory, RBI licence registries, NPCI TPAP/PPI lists, SEBI registers, IRDAI lists,
> FIU VASP registers and company sources, in four saturation rounds (parallel research agents per round).
> This is a living document — the Indian fintech ecosystem changes weekly, so entries will drift.
> Methodology: four research rounds (round 1: payments / wealth-insurance-crypto / lending-banking /
> cross-border-regtech-startups; round 2: RBI registries / insurance ecosystem / capital markets /
> SME-lending & microfinance / new startups; round 3: BFSI software & rails / assisted finance & group
> fintechs / global players; round 4: FIU crypto registers / curated-list cross-checks) were merged and
> deduplicated by company name.
> **1386 unique companies**, **1521 raw entries** (135 duplicates merged).

## Coverage note
- Includes RBI-licensed entities (PA-O, PA-CB, PPI, NBFC-P2P, TReDS, payments banks, SFBs, AA, WLA,
  MTSS), NPCI TPAPs, SEBI-registered brokers/OBPP/PMS/RIA, IRDAI-regulated insurers/brokers/TPAs,
  plus funded startups and global players serving India.
- "All fintech companies" in the strictest sense is not achievable — the ecosystem includes thousands of
  unregistered startups that launch weekly. This captures every company surfaced by exhaustive public-web
  and registry sweeps.
- Global players serving India are listed in their relevant cluster sections.
